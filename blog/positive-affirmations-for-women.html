60 positive affirmations for women

November 23, 2026 · Affirmosa blog

The best affirmations don't sound like a poster. They sound like something you'd actually say to a friend, and then, finally, to yourself. These 60 are short, warm and written in plain words.

How to use them: read the list and notice which lines make you pause. Those are the ones to keep. Say one in the morning, write one in your notes, or put one on your Lock Screen where you'll see it all day.

Affirmations for confidence

  • I believe in myself.
  • I walk into every room with confidence.
  • My voice matters.
  • I trust my judgment.
  • I bring value wherever I go.
  • I am good at what I do, and it shows.
  • I use my voice without fear.
  • I rise to every challenge.
  • I am ready for my next big opportunity.
  • I trust myself to handle whatever comes.

Self-love affirmations for women

  • I am worthy of love and respect.
  • I treat myself with love and respect.
  • I am proud of who I am.
  • I love who I am becoming.
  • I am valuable exactly as I am.
  • I know my worth, and I don't settle.
  • I deserve to be treated well.
  • I am worthy of rest, joy and love.
  • There is no one else like me.
  • I choose me, every single day.

For money, success and abundance

  • I am worthy of wealth.
  • Money comes to me easily and often.
  • I deserve to be paid well.
  • My income keeps growing.
  • I always have more than enough.
  • I am building real, lasting wealth.
  • I make money doing what I love.
  • My dream life is on its way to me.
  • I deserve a life beyond my wildest dreams.
  • I am the creator of my own dream life.

For love and relationships

  • I am deeply loved.
  • I am worthy of a great love.
  • My heart is open to love.
  • I am loved for exactly who I am.
  • I deserve a love that feels like home.
  • I attract kind people.
  • I choose friends who are good for me.
  • The love I give comes back to me.

For calm and positivity

  • My mind is calm and clear.
  • I let go of what I can't control.
  • My peace is stronger than my worries.
  • I choose hope.
  • I see the good in everything.
  • Good things are happening to me today.
  • I radiate positive energy.
  • I love my life.

Gratitude affirmations

  • I am thankful for the people who love me.
  • I am thankful for how far I have come.
  • I live with a grateful heart.
  • My life is full of blessings.
  • I am grateful for exactly where I am.
  • Life is good, and I am grateful.
  • I appreciate myself today.

Christian affirmations for women

  • God is with me.
  • I am safe in God's hands.
  • God goes before me in everything I do.
  • I trust God's plan for my life.
  • God's timing is perfect.
  • What God has for me is on its way.
  • God is working everything out for my good.

Questions

What are good positive affirmations for women?

Short, positive I statements that feel true to you, such as: I believe in myself, I am worthy of love and respect, or: I always have more than enough.

How do I choose the right affirmation?

Read a list and keep the lines that make you pause. One affirmation you believe is worth more than ten you skip.

When should I say affirmations?

Any time, but mornings and before bed are easy habits. Seeing one on your phone's Lock Screen keeps it in front of you all day.
