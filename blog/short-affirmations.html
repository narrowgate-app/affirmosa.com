60 short affirmations

November 23, 2026 · Affirmosa blog

A short affirmation is easier to remember, easier to repeat and easier to believe. Every line here is a few words long, so you can say it in one breath.

How to use them: pick one for the day. Say it when you wake up, when you wash your hands, when you pick up your phone. Short lines also fit where long ones don't: a sticky note, a phone widget, the top of a to-do list.

Questions

What is a good short affirmation?

One you can say in a breath and believe, such as: I am enough, My voice matters, or: I choose hope.

Do short affirmations work as well as long ones?

Short lines are easier to remember and repeat, which is what makes an affirmation stick. Choose the ones that feel true to you.

How many short affirmations should I use?

One to three at a time. Keep the same ones for a week or so before changing them.

Where can I put a short affirmation so I see it?

On a sticky note, a mirror, or your phone. An affirmation widget, like Affirmosa's, shows one on your iPhone Lock Screen.
