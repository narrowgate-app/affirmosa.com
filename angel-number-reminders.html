A sign at 11:11

Affirmosa's widgets can change at angel numbers. Look at your phone at 11:11, 2:22 or 3:33 and a new affirmation is waiting.

Angel-number widgets

Set any widget to change at angel numbers: 11:11, 1:11, 2:22, 3:33, 4:44 and 5:55. Pick the categories it shows, and the photo behind it on the Home Screen.

Angel-number reminders

Turn on angel-number reminders and a notification arrives at 11:11 with a fresh affirmation. Tap it to see your sign full screen, then save or share it. Reminders during the night are skipped, so nobody is woken up.

Gentle reminders all day

  • Morning, midday, evening, or your own times.
  • A different affirmation each time, from the categories you choose.
  • Soft sounds, or silent.

Questions

Is there an app that shows affirmations at 11:11?

Yes. Affirmosa's widgets can change at 11:11, 2:22, 3:33 and other angel numbers, and it can send an optional reminder at 11:11.

Which angel numbers does it use?

11:11 in the morning and evening, plus 1:11, 2:22, 3:33, 4:44 and 5:55 in the afternoon.

Will reminders wake me up at night?

No. Night-time angel numbers are skipped.
