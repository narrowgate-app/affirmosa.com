Your words, everywhere you look

Affirmosa is an affirmations app for iPhone. It puts a fresh affirmation on your Lock Screen, your Home Screen and your wallpaper, and changes it at 11:11, 2:22 and 3:33, every hour, or once each morning.

Angel numbers

Affirmation wallpapers

Daily affirmations you choose

Pick what you want to hear: Motivation, Confidence, Manifest, Wealth & Abundance, Positivity, Overthinking, Self-love, Love & relationships, Gratitude and Faith. Every line is written for Affirmosa, plain and confident: "I am creating the life of my dreams." "Something wonderful is about to happen."

Private by design

  • No account and no ads.
  • Your name, your answers and the lines you write stay on your phone.
  • Write your own affirmations, or reword any line.

Questions

What is Affirmosa?

An affirmations app for iPhone with Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, affirmation wallpapers, reminders and a daily feed of positive affirmations.

Is Affirmosa free?

Affirmosa Premium starts with a 3-day free trial, then $2.99 per week. Cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Everything you choose stays on your iPhone.
